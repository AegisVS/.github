# Aegis Networks

Distinct worlds. Shared foundation.

Aegis Networks is home to a group of Vintage Story servers, each with its own team and its own way to play, from PvP arenas to long-running civilization worlds. Behind them sits one foundation: a single account across the network, a shared panel, and people who support every server's team.
